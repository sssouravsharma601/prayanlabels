About Prayan Labels & Craft

A decade of making labels and latkans that brands trust — built on in-house production, honest quality and fast delivery.

10+ Years of Craft
Our Story

Started Small,
Built on Quality

Prayan Labels & Craft started over a decade ago with one simple idea — garment brands deserve label accessories that are as carefully made as the garments they go on. Too many orders were going to middlemen who compromised quality and blew timelines.

We set up our own production unit, brought every process in-house, and built relationships directly with brands — big and small. Today we supply latkans, labels and craft accessories to 500+ fashion brands, boutiques and exporters across India.

The same attention we gave to order #1, we give to every order today.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Quality Above All

Every batch goes through a pre-production sample sign-off and a final QC before dispatch. We don't ship anything we wouldn't put on our own garments.

Respect for Deadlines

Fashion runs on tight timelines. We plan production schedules carefully and communicate proactively — no surprises, no delays.

Long-Term Relationships

Most of our clients have been with us for years. We invest in understanding your brand, your production cycles and your standards.

100% In-House

Design, weaving, printing, assembly and packing — all under one roof. No outsourcing means full accountability on every order.

How We Work

From Your Brief to Your Doorstep

1

Artwork & Briefing

You share your logo, colour refs, size and material preferences. We prepare a digital artwork proof for your review.

2

Physical Sample

A production sample is made and dispatched within 48 hours. You approve before we start bulk manufacturing.

3

Bulk Production

Production runs on our in-house looms, printers and winding machines. In-line checks at every stage.

4

QC & Dispatch

Final manual quality check, packing and dispatch PAN India. Tracking shared immediately after shipment.

Our Facility

In-House. Every Step.

Our production unit runs automatic looms for woven labels, latkan winding machines, and label printing presses — all calibrated for consistent output across every batch.

  • Automatic narrow-fabric looms for woven labels
  • Latkan winding & tassel assembly machines
  • Offset + digital label printing presses
  • In-house die-cutting and lamination
  • Final QC is always manual — no machine shortcuts
