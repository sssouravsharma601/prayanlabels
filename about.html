Our Story

The Label Behind the Brand

Prayan Labels was founded with a singular focus: give garment manufacturers and fashion brands access to high-quality labels and latkans without compromising on turnaround time or minimum order quantity.

Over more than a decade, we've grown from a small label-making unit into a full-service garment labeling manufacturer serving brands across India. From ethnic wear latkans to export-ready wash care labels — our production lines handle the entire spectrum.

Every order — 500 pieces or 5 lakh — gets the same attention to detail. That's the standard our clients have held us to since day one.

  • In-house design support — share your logo, we handle the rest
  • Sample approval before bulk production — always
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs
  • Direct communication — no middlemen, no delays

Quality Standards

Quality Is Not Negotiable

We run pre-production and in-process checks on every batch. Colour accuracy, thread count, print registration and dimensional tolerance are verified against approved samples.

Our wash care labels are printed with fade-resistant inks and tested for colorfastness across multiple wash cycles — so your label stays legible long after the garment leaves the store.
