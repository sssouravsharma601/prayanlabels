Our Products

A complete range of garment labeling solutions — crafted with precision, delivered with consistency.

Signature Product

Latkans

The defining embellishment of ethnic Indian wear. Custom shapes, premium threads and brand imprinting — perfect for sarees, kurtis, lehengas and dupattas.

Ethnic Wear

Thread Latkans

Multi-strand silk and polyester thread latkans with custom colours and logo charms. Available in all colour combinations to match your garment palette.

Premium

Zari Latkans

Gold and silver zari thread latkans for bridal and festive ethnic collections. Rich, lustrous finish — a mark of premium craftsmanship.

Custom

Logo Charm Latkans

Metal or acrylic charm with your brand logo, attached to thread or cord of your choice. MOQ from 500 pcs.

Brand Identity

Woven Labels

High-definition woven labels that carry your brand through every wash. Damask, satin, taffeta and canvas weaves available.

Most Popular

Damask Woven Labels

Ultra-fine weave for small text and intricate logo detail. Incredibly soft against skin — ideal for neckbands and inner care labels.

Luxury

Satin Woven Labels

Glossy satin weave with a premium look and feel. The label of choice for luxury and designer apparel.

Everyday

Taffeta Woven Labels

Economical and durable — perfect for casual wear, sportswear and high-volume garment production.

Print Solutions

Printed Tags & Labels

Full-colour digital and offset printed labels — custom sizes, shapes and finishes on any substrate.

Retail

Price Tags

Custom printed price tags with barcode, MRP and product detail — paper or synthetic, single or double sided.

Branding

Brand Main Labels

Printed neck labels and main brand labels with logo, size and country-of-origin. Domestic and export ready.

Export

Barcode Stickers

EAN-13, UPC and QR code labels on rolls. Thermal and digital print options in any quantity.

Compliance

Wash Care Labels

Woven

Woven Care Labels

Printed

Export Ready

Multi-language Care Labels

Specialty Finish Tags

Kraft / Recycled Tags
