Configuration Guide

Available Fold & Cut Types

Select the fold that suits your garment type. Not sure? Our team will recommend the best option for your application and sewing method.

Center Fold

Folded lengthwise at center. Standard for neck & collar labels.

End Fold

Both raw ends folded inward. Clean finish for side-seam labels.

Loop Fold

Hanging loop for belt loops, bags & zipper tag applications.

Mitre / Manhattan

All four corners folded. Premium finish for luxury & export garments.

Straight Cut

Flat sew-on label. Most economical choice for high-volume orders.

Also available: Ultrasonic-cut edges, heat-seal backing, iron-on adhesive, and laser-cut precision edges on request.

Tagless & Skin-Friendly

Heat Transfer Labels

Applied directly onto fabric with heat and pressure — no stitching, no irritation, no bulge. The preferred choice for athleisure, innerwear, kids wear and premium sportswear where comfort matters.

  • No sewing — applied with a heat press in seconds
  • Flat, skin-smooth finish — zero irritation even for infants
  • Works on cotton, polyester, nylon and blended fabrics
  • Wash-resistant — survives 50+ machine wash cycles
  • Full-colour printing + metallic effect options available
  • Brand logo, size, care symbols — all on one label
