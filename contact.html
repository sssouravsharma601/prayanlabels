Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
MOQ varies by product. Latkans start from 500 pieces per design. Woven labels from 500 pcs. Hang tags and printed labels from 1,000 pcs. Contact us for smaller trial quantities — we try to accommodate new clients.
How long does it take to get a sample? +
Physical samples are dispatched within 48 hours of artwork approval. Digital proofs are sent same day in most cases.
What format should I send my logo in? +
Vector formats (AI, EPS, SVG) are ideal for woven labels and printing. High-resolution PNG or PDF also work. If you only have a JPG, share it and our design team will prepare a production-ready file.
Do you deliver outside India? +
We primarily serve PAN India clients. For international orders, please contact us directly to discuss shipping arrangements and timelines.
Can I get custom colours and sizes for latkans? +
Absolutely. Latkans are fully customisable — thread colour, charm type, tassel length, bead style and more. Share a reference image or Pantone code and we'll match it.
What are the payment terms? +
For new clients, we typically work on 50% advance and 50% before dispatch. Repeat clients with good order history can discuss credit terms with us directly.
