Common Questions

FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+

MOQ varies by product. Most printed labels and latkans start from 500–1000 pieces. Woven labels typically have an MOQ of 1000 pieces. Contact us with your specific requirement and we'll work it out.

How long does sampling take?+

Physical samples are ready within 48–72 hours of artwork approval and advance payment. Digital proofs are shared within 24 hours.

What artwork formats do you accept?+

We prefer vector files — AI, EPS or high-resolution PDF. For woven labels, a JPG/PNG reference is fine to start — our design team converts it to a weave-ready format.

Do you supply pan-India? What about exports?+

Yes — we ship across India via courier. For export orders, contact us directly to discuss freight, documentation and compliance label requirements.

What is your payment policy?+

50% advance and 50% before dispatch for new clients. Regular clients may be eligible for credit terms. We accept bank transfer, UPI and RTGS/NEFT.
